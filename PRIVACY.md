# Apex Office privacy

Apex Office opens, edits and saves documents on your computer. It has no account,
no sign-in and no AI features, and it does not send your documents, your usage or
anything else about you to anyone.

- **No usage reporting.** The program does not collect or transmit analytics.
- **No automatic updates.** The program does not check for or download updates by
  itself. A new version is a new installer you choose to run.
- **Documents stay local.** Opening, editing, searching, converting, printing and
  recognising text (OCR) all run on your computer. Search keeps a local index of
  your files' names and text in the program's data folder; delete the folder to
  remove it.
- **Things you start yourself.** Read Out Loud uses the voice built into Windows.
  Scan to PDF uses your scanner's own driver. Printing sends pages to the printer
  you choose. A link you click opens in your browser.
